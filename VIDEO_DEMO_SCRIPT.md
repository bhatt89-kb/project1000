# 🎬 Video Demo Script - LegalLens Lite Hackathon Presentation

**Duration:** 3-5 minutes  
**Format:** Screen recording + voiceover  
**Tone:** Professional, friendly, problem-focused

---

## 🎯 Script Outline

### **Opening (0:00 - 0:30) - Hook with Problem**

**[Screen: Show statistics/headlines about legal disputes]**

**Voiceover:**
> "Did you know that 85% of people sign rental agreements without fully reading them? Every year, millions lose thousands of dollars to hidden clauses they never understood. Meet Sarah."

**[Screen: Show animated character looking at a long contract]**

> "Sarah just found her dream apartment. But this 25-page lease agreement is full of legal jargon she doesn't understand. Should she spend $500 on a lawyer? Wait 3 weeks for an appointment? Or just... sign and hope for the best?"

**[Screen: Fade to LegalLens Lite logo]**

> "There's a better way. Introducing LegalLens Lite."

---

### **Section 1 (0:30 - 1:30) - The Solution**

**[Screen: Open LegalLens Lite homepage]**

**Voiceover:**
> "LegalLens Lite is a free, AI-powered platform that analyzes legal documents in seconds. Let me show you how it works."

**[Screen: Demo the upload process]**

**Action:**
1. Click "Choose File"
2. Select sample rental agreement PDF
3. Click "Analyze Document"
4. Show progress bar (2-3 seconds)

**Voiceover:**
> "Simply upload your contract - PDF or text file up to 5 MB. Our AI analyzes it in under 3 seconds."

**[Screen: Results appear with tabs]**

> "And instantly, you get a complete breakdown."

---

### **Section 2 (1:30 - 2:30) - Key Features Demo**

#### **Feature 1: Risk Assessment (1:30 - 1:50)**

**[Screen: Click on Overview tab, zoom into Risk Assessment section]**

**Voiceover:**
> "First, a risk assessment. This document scores 7 out of 10 - that's HIGH risk. Let's see why."

**[Screen: Scroll through risk stats]**

> "It found 8 risky clauses, including 3 high-priority issues. You can see exactly what categories: liability, termination, penalties."

#### **Feature 2: Document Summary (1:50 - 2:05)**

**[Screen: Scroll to Summary section]**

**Voiceover:**
> "Next, key terms extracted automatically. Monthly rent: 18,000 rupees. Security deposit: 50,000. Notice period: 2 months. Everything at a glance."

#### **Feature 3: Detailed Analysis (2:05 - 2:30)**

**[Screen: Click "Detailed Analysis" tab]**

**Voiceover:**
> "Here's where it gets powerful. Every flagged clause is explained in plain English."

**[Screen: Click on a high-risk finding]**

> "For example: 'Automatic Renewal' - Risk score 9/10. This clause means your contract renews automatically unless you give 60 days notice. That could lock you in for another year if you forget. Page 12, Clause 7."

**[Screen: Show another finding]**

> "Or this: 'Unlimited Liability' - you're responsible for ALL damages, even from natural disasters. That's not fair."

---

### **Section 3 (2:30 - 3:15) - Comparison Feature**

**[Screen: Click "Compare" tab]**

**Voiceover:**

**[Screen: Upload second document]**

**Action:**
1. Click "Upload second document"

3. Show comparison results

**Voiceover:**

> Clear winner: Apartment B saves you money AND gives you more flexibility."

## 🎬 Production Notes

### **Visuals to Prepare**
1. ✅ Sample rental agreement PDF (5-10 pages)
2. ✅ Second rental agreement for comparison
3. ✅ Logo/branding assets
4. ✅ Impact statistics graphics
5. ✅ Character illustration (Sarah)
6. ✅ Clean browser window (no tabs/bookmarks visible)

### **Screen Recording Setup**
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps minimum
- **Audio:** Clear microphone (no background noise)
- **Cursor:** Highlight cursor movements
- **Zoom:** Use 125% browser zoom for readability

### **Editing Checklist**
- [ ] Add background music (subtle, non-distracting)
- [ ] Add text overlays for key statistics
- [ ] Use smooth transitions between sections
- [ ] Add captions/subtitles (accessibility)
- [ ] Speed up slow parts (1.25x-1.5x)
- [ ] Add call-to-action cards at end
- [ ] Export in multiple formats (MP4, WebM)

### **Voice Over Tips**
- ✅ **Pace:** Slightly slower than normal speech
- ✅ **Tone:** Professional but friendly
- ✅ **Energy:** Enthusiastic but not over-the-top
- ✅ **Pauses:** Brief pauses after key points
- ✅ **Emphasis:** Stress important numbers/features

### **Alternative B-Roll Ideas**
- Time-lapse of documents being analyzed
- Animation showing how the AI works
- Split-screen showing user reaction
- Statistics overlays with sources
- Testimonial quotes from beta users
